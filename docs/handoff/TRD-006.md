# TRD-006 — Paper and shadow outcome analytics

## Summary

Closed paper and shadow trades are scored in Python with `Decimal`. The model does not do the arithmetic. `trading.crypto.outcomes` and `trading.nse.outcomes` GET `/trades` (one page, limit 500) and return a fact sheet. A narration has to show every book's sample size and period, copy the under-30 warning when the sheet has it, and may not cite a number the sheet does not contain. Research rows and open rows are not results. Books are split by mode and currency.

Definitions locked by the tests:

- Win: `pnl > 0`. Loss: `pnl < 0`. Zero stays in the sample and breaks a streak.
- Win rate = wins / sample size. Average win and average loss are means; average loss is the positive magnitude. Expectancy is the mean pnl. Profit factor is gross profit / gross loss, and is undefined when there is no loss.
- Max drawdown walks cumulative pnl from zero in `(closed_at, id)` order. It is `peak - equity` in pnl currency.
- Fewer than 30 included trades is a small sample. The figures are still returned.

## Assumptions

- Branch is `jarvis/trd-006`, cut from `jarvis/trd-005` (`950c01c`), not from `main`. `main` does not contain the trading-v1 trade model these figures are computed from. The pull request targets `jarvis/trd-005` so the diff is only TRD-006. TRD-005 was already committed and pushed.
- The book currency is `pnl.currency`. Fees are not subtracted again.
- Paper and shadow are separate books even in the same currency. Research is excluded.
- A page with `next_cursor` is flagged incomplete. The client does not invent a cursor query the contract does not define, and it does not walk past the 500-trade page.
- The under-30 warning is the sentence `Sample is below 30 trades; these figures are not a stable edge.`
- The commit trailer `Co-authored-by` is inserted by the environment. It was left in place because rewriting history is not allowed. The message itself was written without that line.

## Known gaps

- Nothing points the outcomes tools at a live engine. With the base URL unset they report unavailable and do not invent a win rate.
- Stats are for one page. A book larger than 500 closed trades is marked incomplete rather than silently treated as the full history.
- Sharpe is not computed. The performance endpoint on the engine is not an input; these figures come only from `/trades`.
- A narration can omit a metric. It cannot invent one, and it cannot omit sample size, period, or a warning the sheet already carries.

## Security impact

- The new tools are GET-only reads. Capability is READ and risk is SAFE. They do not place, cancel, or size an order.
- Research and open rows cannot be talked into the win rate.
- A rejected narration is not stored, same as the TRD-005 summary check.
- No new secret. A missing base URL reports the variable name.
